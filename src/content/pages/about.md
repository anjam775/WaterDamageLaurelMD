Laurel Dry Pros helps homeowners, renters, landlords and businesses recover from water damage. We remove water, dry buildings, clean up sewage, remove mold and handle the water and smoke left after a fire. Our phone is answered 24/7, and we have more than 10 years in the water damage business.

## What we do

We focus on water damage restoration in Laurel and its neighbors in Prince George's, Howard, Anne Arundel and Montgomery counties. That focus lets us learn the local patterns: frozen pipes in Beltsville crawl spaces, sump failures in Burtonsville basements, shared-wall leaks in Greenbelt row homes and Patuxent River flooding in Old Town Laurel. ## How we work

!checks
- **We answer 24/7.** Water emergencies don't keep business hours.
- **We explain before we start.** You get a clear scope and a written estimate for work beyond emergency steps.
- **We measure, not guess.** Moisture meters, thermal imaging and daily readings decide when a home is dry.
- **We document.** Photos, moisture maps and drying logs support your insurance claim.
- **We tell you what we don't do.** Plumbing, electrical and roofing repairs need their own licensed trades, and we say so.
