Document: Accessibility statement for Laurel Dry Pros (waterdamagerestorationlaurelmd.com). Owner: Laurel Dry Pros. Effective November 1, 2026.

## Our goal

We want everyone to be able to use this website. We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, Level AA.

## What we've done

!checks
- Text and buttons meet AA color contrast.
- Every page has one main heading and a logical heading order.
- Menus, buttons and forms work with a keyboard, with a visible focus outline.
- A "Skip to content" link appears at the top of each page.
- Images have text alternatives, and decorative icons are hidden from screen readers.
- Pages work on phones, tablets and desktops, and respect reduced-motion settings.
- Phone numbers are tap-to-call.

## Known limits

The optional map on our contact page comes from Google Maps and may not be fully accessible. Our service area is also listed in text on the same page.

## Report a barrier

If something on this site is hard to use, call [(301) 368-0221](tel:+13013680221) or use our [contact page](/contact/). Tell us the page and the problem, and we'll work to fix it. We can also give you the information by phone.
