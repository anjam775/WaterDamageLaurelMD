## What to do right now when your water heater is leaking

1. **Shut off the cold water supply.** Turn the valve on the cold inlet pipe at the top of the tank clockwise. If you can't find it or it won't turn, shut off the main water valve.
2. **Turn off the energy source.** For electric heaters, switch off the breaker. For gas, turn the gas control knob to "off" or "pilot."
3. **Stay safe.** Don't touch electrical parts while standing in water. If you smell gas, leave the house and call 911 and the gas company.
4. **Contain the water.** Use towels, a wet vac or a pump to stop it spreading into finished rooms.
5. **Drain the tank if needed.** A plumber can drain it through the drain valve to stop the leak. The water may be very hot.
6. **Take photos** of the heater, the label and the water damage.
7. **Call a licensed plumber** for the heater and a water damage company if water reached carpet, drywall or other rooms.

## Why is my water heater leaking?

| Where the leak is | Likely cause | Usual fix |
|---|---|---|
| Top: cold or hot water pipes | Loose fitting or corroded connector | Tighten or replace fittings |
| Top or side: T&P valve or its pipe | Too much pressure or heat, or a worn valve | Plumber checks pressure and replaces the valve |
| Bottom: drain valve | Valve loose or worn | Tighten or replace the drain valve |
| Bottom: from under the tank | Tank rusted through | Replace the water heater |
| Drips with no clear source | Condensation on a cold tank | Usually goes away once the tank heats up |
| Around the gas burner area | Tank leak or condensation | Plumber inspection |

## Is a leaking water heater dangerous?

It can be. The T&P valve, short for temperature and pressure relief valve, opens to release water if the tank gets too hot or the pressure gets too high. Water from that valve may mean the tank is overheating or the pressure is too high, which a licensed plumber should check right away. Water near a gas burner or wiring is also a hazard. And a tank that rusts through can let go suddenly.

## Why water heaters fail

Most tank water heaters are steel tanks lined with glass. Over time, sediment settles at the bottom and minerals and rust wear down the lining. A part called the anode rod protects the tank from corrosion, but it wears out too. Many tanks last around 8 to 12 years. The U.S. Department of Energy notes that regular maintenance helps tank water heaters last longer and work better.

## What the water does to your home

A failed water heater often sits in a basement, garage or utility closet. The water spreads under walls into finished rooms, soaks carpet pad and wicks up drywall. In a closet on an upper floor, it can come through the ceiling below.

The EPA says wet materials dried within 24 to 48 hours usually won't grow mold. Fans alone can't dry carpet pad or the inside of walls. Our guide on [water mitigation vs. restoration](/blog/water-mitigation-vs-restoration/) explains what professional drying involves.

## How to prevent a water heater leak

!checks
- Put the water heater in a drain pan piped to a floor drain, especially on upper floors.
- Add a water leak alarm or an automatic shutoff valve.
- Have a plumber flush the tank and check the anode rod as recommended by the maker.
- Test the T&P valve once a year, or have a plumber do it.
- Check the age on the label. Plan to replace an older tank before it fails.
- Keep the area around the tank clear so leaks are easy to spot.

## Does insurance cover a leaking water heater?

The water damage from a sudden tank failure is often covered by a homeowners policy. The water heater itself usually isn't, because it failed from age and wear. The Maryland Insurance Administration also notes that many policies won't pay to fix the pipe or appliance that caused the leak. ## Licensing in Maryland

Water heater replacement is plumbing work, and gas connections need a qualified, licensed professional. Maryland's Department of Labor notes that a home improvement contractor license doesn't authorize plumbing, electrical or HVAC work. Ask to see the plumber's license.
