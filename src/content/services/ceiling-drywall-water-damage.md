!quick Ceiling and drywall water damage repair in Laurel, MD, means finding where the water came from, drying the ceiling or wall cavity, and replacing only what can't be saved. Wet drywall that dries within 24 to 48 hours can often stay. Sagging, crumbling or moldy drywall should come out and be rebuilt.

Water travels before it shows. A leak from a second-floor tub, an AC drain pan in the attic or an ice dam on the roof can run along joists and appear several feet away. Laurel Dry Pros traces the water, dries the cavity and repairs the ceiling or wall so the stain doesn't come back.

## What ceiling and drywall repair includes

!checks

## Signs and sources of ceiling water damage

| Sign | Likely source | What it tells us |
|---|---|---|
| Yellow or brown ring stain | Slow drip from a pipe, tub or roof | Water has wet and dried more than once. |
| Bulging or sagging drywall | Pooled water above the ceiling | Urgent. The panel can fail. |
| Peeling paint or bubbling | Moisture trapped behind paint | Drywall is wet now. |
| Stain under a bathroom | Tub, shower pan, toilet seal or drain | Needs a plumber to fix the source. |
| Stain near an outside wall in winter | Ice dam or frozen pipe | Check the roof edge and wall cavity. |
| Stain below the attic in summer | AC drain pan or condensate line | HVAC drain needs service. |
| Dark spots or fuzzy growth | Long-term moisture | Mold. See [mold remediation](/services/mold-remediation/). |

## Our ceiling and wall repair process

1. **Make it safe.** We drain a bulging ceiling into buckets with a small hole and turn off nearby fixtures.
2. **Find the source.** We trace the water path and tell you whether a plumber, roofer or HVAC tech is needed.
3. **Map the moisture.** Thermal imaging and meters show how far the water spread.
4. **Remove what can't dry.** Sagging drywall, wet insulation and moldy material come out.
5. **Dry the cavity.** Wall cavity dryers, dehumidifiers and air movers run until readings are dry.
6. **Check for mold.** We look at framing and the back of nearby drywall.
7. **Rebuild.** New drywall is hung, finished, primed and painted to match.

## Why water climbs drywall

Drywall is gypsum pressed between paper. It soaks up water through wicking, the way a paper towel does. Water on a floor can rise a foot or more inside a wall. The paper facing also feeds mold. That's why we measure above the visible stain before deciding what to cut.

## DIY vs. calling a pro

A small, dry, old stain with a fixed source can be primed and painted. Call a pro if the stain is growing, the drywall is soft or sagging, the leak source is unknown, or you smell must. Water above a ceiling can't be dried with a fan from below.

## Licensing and insurance notes for Maryland

In Maryland, drywall and finish repairs on homes are home improvement work. Contractors must hold a Maryland Home Improvement Commission license, and the state notes that a home improvement license does not cover plumbing, electrical or HVAC work. Those need their own licensed trades.

For insurance, sudden leaks from plumbing are often covered. Long-term, repeated seepage often is not. The Maryland Insurance Administration also notes many policies won't pay to repair the pipe or drain itself. Report the damage soon after you find it and take photos before repairs.
