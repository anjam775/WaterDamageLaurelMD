Ceiling & Drywall Water Damage Repair in Laurel, MD

A brown stain, a bulging ceiling or soft drywall means water is trapped inside. We find it, dry it and put the room back together.

Quick answer: Ceiling and drywall water damage repair in Laurel, MD, means finding where the water came from, drying the ceiling or wall cavity, and replacing only what can't be saved. Wet drywall that dries within 24 to 48 hours can often stay. Sagging, crumbling or moldy drywall should come out and be rebuilt.

Water travels before it shows. A leak from a second-floor tub, an AC drain pan in the attic or an ice dam on the roof can run along joists and appear several feet away. Laurel Dry Pros traces the water, dries the cavity and repairs the ceiling or wall so the stain doesn't come back.

What ceiling and drywall repair includes

  • Thermal imaging and moisture readings to map the wet area
  • Safe draining of a bulging ceiling before it falls
  • Removal of wet or sagging drywall and wet insulation
  • Wall cavity drying to save drywall where possible
  • Dehumidifiers and air movers with daily readings
  • Mold check of framing and the back of drywall
  • New drywall, tape, mud and texture matched to the room
  • Stain-blocking primer and paint
  • Trim and baseboard reset
  • Photos and notes for your insurance claim

Our ceiling and wall repair process

  1. Make it safe. We drain a bulging ceiling into buckets with a small hole and turn off nearby fixtures.
  2. Find the source. We trace the water path and tell you whether a plumber, roofer or HVAC tech is needed.
  3. Map the moisture. Thermal imaging and meters show how far the water spread.
  4. Remove what can't dry. Sagging drywall, wet insulation and moldy material come out.
  5. Dry the cavity. Wall cavity dryers, dehumidifiers and air movers run until readings are dry.
  6. Check for mold. We look at framing and the back of nearby drywall.
  7. Rebuild. New drywall is hung, finished, primed and painted to match.

Why water climbs drywall

Our guide to the signs of hidden water in walls shows what to look for before a stain appears.

Drywall is gypsum pressed between paper. It soaks up water through wicking, the way a paper towel does. Water on a floor can rise a foot or more inside a wall. The paper facing also feeds mold. That's why we measure above the visible stain before deciding what to cut.

Tools for hidden water in walls and ceilings

Tool What it does
Thermal imaging camera Finds cooler, wet areas behind paint. We confirm with a meter.
Pinless moisture meter Scans drywall without damage.
Pin moisture meter Measures wood framing and the core of drywall.
Wall cavity drying system Pushes dry air through small holes in walls and ceilings.
HEPA vacuum Cleans dust and spores during tear-out.

DIY vs. calling a pro

A small, dry, old stain with a fixed source can be primed and painted. Call a pro if the stain is growing, the drywall is soft or sagging, the leak source is unknown, or you smell must. Water above a ceiling can't be dried with a fan from below.

Licensing and insurance notes for Maryland

In Maryland, drywall and finish repairs on homes are home improvement work. Contractors must hold a Maryland Home Improvement Commission license, and the state notes that a home improvement license does not cover plumbing, electrical or HVAC work. Those need their own licensed trades.

For insurance, sudden leaks from plumbing are often covered. Long-term, repeated seepage often is not. The Maryland Insurance Administration also notes many policies won't pay to repair the pipe or drain itself. Report the damage soon after you find it and take photos before repairs.

Ceiling & Drywall Repair questions

Is a sagging ceiling dangerous?

Yes. A bulge means water is pooled above the drywall, and wet drywall can give way. Keep people out of the room, put a bucket under it and turn off power to nearby lights if you can do it safely. Then call.

Can I just paint over a water stain?

Not until you know the leak is fixed and the area is dry. Paint can hide a wet spot for a while, but mold can keep growing behind it. Use a stain-blocking primer only after the moisture meter reads dry.

Who should I call for water damage in a ceiling?

Call a plumber or roofer to stop the leak, and a water damage company to dry and repair the ceiling. Laurel Dry Pros handles the drying and repair, and we can tell you which trade the source needs.

Does wet drywall always have to be replaced?

No. Drywall wet with clean water that is dried quickly can often be saved. Drywall that sagged, crumbled, got wet with dirty water or stayed wet long enough to grow mold should be replaced.

How long before I can repaint?

After drying is confirmed with a moisture meter, usually within a few days of the leak. New drywall needs mud, sanding and primer, which adds a few more days.
